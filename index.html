    • I am an Assistant Research Professor working in the Bioinspired Advanced Manufacturing Laboratory at the University of Maryland. In 2022-24, I was in the inaugural cohort of MIT's Postdoctoral Fellowship for Engineering Excellence, working in the Portela Group in Mechanical Engineering. I did my PhD in the Solheim Additive Manufacturing Lab and the Transformative Robotics Lab in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, UW Seattle, with a concurrent MS in Applied Math (2018). I received my Bachelors in Physics at Princeton University in 2014.

    My research is in the area of additive manufacturing of metamaterials using micro- and macro-scale printing of polymers, particularly as applied to soft and continuum robotics. I'm interested in the interaction of computer representations of geometry and physical fabrication, and how we can expand what we can acheive through material and structural behavior by using computational design and fabrication to create programmable or tunable materials. My work spans parametric design, simulation, fabrication process, and experimental validation.
