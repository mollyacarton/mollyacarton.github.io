    • I am an Assistant Research Professor working in the Bioinspired Advanced Manufacturing Laboratory at University of Maryland. In 2022-24, I held a Postdoctoral Fellowship in the Portela Group in Mechanical Engineering at MIT. I did my PhD in the Transformative Robotics Lab in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, UW Seattle, with a concurrent MS in Applied Math (Spring 2018). I received my Bachelors in Physics at Princeton University in 2014.
